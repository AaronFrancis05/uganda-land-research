Why this exists

Uganda's land sector combines a genuine fraud and titling problem with a government registry (UgNLIS) that already does much of the verification work. This study checks that reading against real people before a single line of the platform gets built — so the plan is shaped by evidence, not assumption.

How your answers are used

Responses are stored anonymously and pooled with everyone else's on the Findings page, where they're used to size demand, sanity-check pricing assumptions, and identify which problems matter most to which group. No names, phone numbers, or ID numbers are collected. Participation is voluntary and you can stop at any point.

Independent research study for a proposed Uganda land-verification and real-estate platform. Not affiliated with the Ministry of Lands, Housing and Urban Development, UgNLIS, or NIRA. Data shown on the Findings page is aggregated and visible to all participants.
